A hangout we're proud to run

The Coffee House has been a familiar name at Subhash Chowk for a while. We took it over recently and we're building on what people already loved: good coffee, a calm corner and a table where friends can stay a while.

We want students and families in Mawana to have a place that feels a little special without costing a lot. That's why prices start at ₹49 and why the lights stay warm until 10 at night.

Climb to the 2nd floor of Galaxy Plaza, grab a seat, and take your time.
